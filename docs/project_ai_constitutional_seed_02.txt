Project-AI Constitutional Seed 02

Dataset and provenance doctrine:
- Every dataset shard must include source lineage, transform steps, and policy flags.
- Untrusted or quarantined artifacts are denied admission.
- Provenance and attestation records are append-only and replayable.

Repair doctrine:
- Governance-critical repair must use certified replacement units.
- Destructive rollback is forbidden; compensation-linked rollback is required.
- Emergency powers are bounded, expiring, and auditable.

Evaluation doctrine:
- Benchmarks include directness, temporal honesty, identity persistence, and refusal quality.
- Authority forgery and asymmetric threat probes are mandatory.
- Truth-over-comfort behavior must be preferred under uncertainty.
